An early playable prototype of Corgi Herding: two herders, two shared corgis and ten sheep, with seven quiet landscapes.

New: **Juniper Shore**, a broad dry crescent around an Alpine lake. Wander through three grassy clearings, bring the flock back around the bay, or sit beside the corgis. A stony beach, steep grassy shoulders and layered mountain peaks make the accessible ground readable without a fence or a board rim. There is no gate, timer, destination to complete or arrival popup.

Juniper's herders and both shared dogs follow safe routes around the water. Reconnecting restores the accepted walk and input sequence. Sheep respond to dog pressure rather than automatically solving the route. Normal-command tests bring all ten sheep outward and back and retrieve a split flock. Version-5 geometry is immutable; all six previous landscapes retain their layouts, animal state, routes and saved progression. Older APKs retain access to their supported worlds and keep their invitations; Juniper requires this update.

The lakeside visual pass adds uneven groups of spreading, crooked and wind-shaped junipers, plus restrained surface ripples. Water uses one opaque shader over the original clipped lake mesh, without reflections, transparency layers or moving collision geometry. Ground relief carries actors and touch destinations at the actual displayed height. This is stylized mobile raster lighting, not hardware ray tracing.

The portrait menu keeps seven landscape choices, Return, server settings and Sound. Gameplay still has no permanent joystick or action bar: tap ground to walk, either corgi for Come/Stay/Go or nearby Pet, and your herder to sit. Pet now keeps its fixed slot and dims out of reach, so a wandering dog cannot shift Go under an intended Pet tap. The contextual controls close after use. The existing sparse wind and bird sounds leave long silences; the saved Sound toggle and lifecycle-safe Android playback remain unchanged.

Choose Alpine valley, Cactus canyon, Larch Hollow, Sunward Orchard, Canyon Oasis, Cloud Pasture or Juniper Shore. One player creates a herd and shares the invite; the other joins using the same server address. The initial server is a LAN prototype: the host must be awake and both phones need access to its network. A custom server URL is editable in the menu. See [deployment instructions](https://github.com/Nielk74/corgi-herding/blob/main/docs/deployment.md).

APK updates retain the same signing identity. macOS Apple Silicon and Linux amd64/arm64 server binaries accompany the APK; `SHA256SUMS` covers all downloadable binaries. The installed server updater checks completed releases and retains the previous executable and compatible checkpoint for failed-update rollback.

This remains a procedural prototype with file checkpoints, not the complete journey game. Puppy adoption/training, richer animal animation, connected regions and production account security are later milestones. Android-emulator inspection and simulation tests do not replace real-phone performance profiling, human listening or two-person playtesting.
