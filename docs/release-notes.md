An early playable prototype of Corgi Herding: two herders, two shared corgis and ten sheep, now with a larger walkable Alpine region and an optional practice meadow.

**Long Alpine valley** is ground you can actually explore, not just a distant backdrop. Broad corridors connect sixteen clearings inside 144-by-192-unit bounds. The camera follows your herder in both directions across the landscape and settles when you stop. Textured grass and rock, worn ground, restrained grass motion and small airborne details add life without changing the authoritative walking surface. Prepared scenery loads from an offline-built, verified cache; actors and touch destinations follow the actual terrain mesh.

**Practice meadow** uses Bellflower Commons' roomy fork and clearings. It is the first selection for a new player without a saved invitation. A gentle, optional guide introduces walking, choosing either corgi, Come, Stay, placing a dog, sheep pressure, backing away and care. One short instruction explains what to try and why. Skip any step, hide the guide, or restart it from settings. Guide visibility is remembered locally for that herd, separately from invitation credentials; the guide does not appear in other landscapes. Lessons use fresh accepted server state after your actions, not optimistic success messages or a timer.

**Place a corgi directly:** press either dog, drag toward a patch of ground, then release to send Go. A ground marker shows the requested destination. Releasing over controls or blocked terrain cancels the drag; it does not replace your herder's existing walk. The familiar tap-to-open Come/Stay/Go controls remain available. Pet keeps its fixed slot and dims when unavailable, so a wandering dog cannot shift Go under an intended tap.

Versioned routes stay server-authoritative. The new region validates complete walking segments and retained anchor queues; reconnecting restores the accepted target and sequence even when a locally predicted tap never reached the server. Remote animals retain safe display routes instead of cutting through steep gaps. Both humans can still command both shared dogs. There is no combat, timer, score or arrival reward, and sheep still respond to animal pressure rather than automatically solving a journey. All seven previously released landscape layouts remain unchanged; older APKs keep their invitations and access to their supported worlds.

The portrait menu keeps nine choices, saved Return, server settings and Sound. Practice meadow and Long Alpine valley occupy the first row; Alpine valley, Cactus canyon, Larch Hollow, Sunward Orchard, Canyon Oasis, Cloud Pasture and Juniper Shore remain available. Contextual controls close after use. Existing sparse wind and birds leave long silences, with the separate saved Sound preference and lifecycle-safe Android playback retained. Lighting remains stylized mobile raster rendering, not hardware ray tracing or baked global illumination. Natural material sources and their CC0 provenance are recorded in the repository.

One player creates a herd and shares the invite; the other joins using the same server address. The initial server is a LAN prototype: the host must be awake and both phones need access to its network. A custom server URL is editable in the menu. See [deployment instructions](https://github.com/Nielk74/corgi-herding/blob/main/docs/deployment.md).

APK updates retain the same signing identity. macOS Apple Silicon and Linux amd64/arm64 server binaries accompany the APK; `SHA256SUMS` covers all downloadable binaries. The installed server updater checks completed releases and retains the previous executable and compatible checkpoint for failed-update rollback.

This remains a procedural prototype with file checkpoints, not the complete journey game. The practice guide teaches controls; it does not add puppy proficiency or adoption progression. Richer animal animation, connected regions and production account security remain later milestones. Android-emulator inspection and simulation tests do not replace real-phone performance profiling, human listening or two-person playtesting.
